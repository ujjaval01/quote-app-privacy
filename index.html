1. Information We Collect

Quote Mantra does not collect personal identity information. We do not require you to create an account or provide a name or email address to use the app.

To deliver notifications, the app uses an anonymous Device Token generated by Firebase. To sync live quotes, the app reads public data from Firebase Cloud Firestore without requiring user authentication.

2. Local Data Storage

The following items are stored locally on your device only:

  • Your "Favorite" quotes list.
  • Your custom created quotes ("My Quotes").
  • Your daily streak counters, longest streak, and active usage dates.
  • Your custom daily reminder time preferences.
  • Your theme preferences (Dark Mode / Light Mode).

This data remains completely under your control on your physical device.

3. Permissions Used

The app may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To send you daily motivational reminders.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To deliver daily reminder notifications at your custom chosen time.
  • App Widgets: To allow you to place a quote widget on your home screen.
  • Internet Access: To receive push notifications, sync live quotes, and handle in-app update checks.

4. Internet Usage

The app requires an internet connection for:

  • Fetching live quote updates from Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Receiving daily thoughts via push notifications.
  • Checking for app updates and processing in-app reviews.
  • Processing AI companion queries if used.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted Google services to power app functionality:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) & Firestore: To deliver notifications and sync live quotes.
  • Google Play Services: For secure in-app updates and rating prompts.
  • Google Gemini AI: For AI companion response processing (queries are processed ephemerally without storing personal profiles).

6. Children's Privacy

Quote Mantra is safe for all ages. We do not collect any personal data from children or adults.

7. Data Security

We follow modern Android security practices (targeting API 36) and use code minification and obfuscation (R8) to protect the application and ensure secure execution.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features. Updates will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date.
