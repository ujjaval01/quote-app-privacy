1. Information We Collect

Quote Mantra does not require you to register an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use its core features.

To deliver notifications, the app may use a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token. To sync live quotes, the app reads public data from Firebase Cloud Firestore. These services process the technical information needed to provide their functionality.

When ads are displayed, Google AdMob may automatically collect or receive information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), advertising or app/device identifiers, interactions with the app and ads (such as taps and video views), and diagnostic/performance information. This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The exact data processed can depend on the SDK version, settings, and features used.

2. Local Data Storage

The following items are stored locally on your device only:

  • Your "Favorite" quotes list.
  • Your custom created quotes ("My Quotes").
  • Your daily streak counters, longest streak, and active usage dates.
  • Your notification inbox history and daily reminder time preferences.
  • Your theme preferences (Dark Mode / Light Mode).

This data remains completely under your control on your physical device.

3. Permissions Used

The app may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To send you daily motivational reminders and milestone alerts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To deliver daily reminder notifications at your custom chosen time.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore daily reminder schedules automatically after your device restarts.
  • App Widgets: To allow you to place a quote widget on your home screen.
  • Internet Access: To receive push notifications, sync live quotes, process AI responses, and handle in-app update checks.

4. Internet Usage

The app requires an internet connection for:

  • Fetching live quote updates from Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Receiving daily thoughts via push notifications.
  • Checking for app updates and processing in-app reviews.
  • Processing AI companion queries if used.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted Google services to power app functionality:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) & Firestore: To deliver notifications and sync live quotes.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK: To display banner ads and optional rewarded video ads. Rewarded ads are shown when you choose to view an ad in exchange for an in-app reward. The SDK may automatically collect and share IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play Services: For secure in-app updates and rating prompts.
  • Generative AI services: To process AI companion requests when you use those features. Information you submit may be sent to the relevant AI service provider to generate a response and is handled under that provider’s terms and privacy practices.

6. Advertising, Ad Personalization & Your Choices

Quote Mantra displays banner ads and may offer rewarded video ads. Banner ads may appear within app screens. Rewarded ads are optional: you decide whether to watch them, and an in-app reward is provided only after the ad provider confirms successful completion.

Google AdMob may use device or advertising identifiers, IP address, ad impressions, taps, video views, app interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and improve its services. Depending on your region, applicable consent requirements, and device or ad settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can review or reset your Android advertising ID and manage relevant ad privacy settings through your Android device settings. Google’s handling of information is also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

We do not control the information independently collected by Google through its advertising SDK. For details about the Google Mobile Ads SDK data practices, see Google’s AdMob data disclosure for Android.

7. Children's Privacy

Quote Mantra is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising availability and ad treatment may depend on the user’s age, applicable law, and the settings configured for the app and its advertising services. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the concern.

8. Data Security

We follow modern Android security practices (targeting API 36) and use code minification and obfuscation (R8) to protect the application and ensure secure execution.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features. Updates will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date.
